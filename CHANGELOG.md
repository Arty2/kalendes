# Changelog

What kalendes gained, newest first, in a few words each. One section per minor
version, headed by the last version it shipped as: a patch rewrites its minor's
heading to the new number and adds its lines there, never a section of its own.
Features only — not fixes, not why, not how; that is the README's. Three lines
to a release, six at the very most. Plain text and `code` spans only.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-03

- What's new: tap the version in the tray, or in Settings, to see what each release brought; it opens by itself once after an update.

## 0.0.85 — 2026-10-03

- Your iCal feeds side by side on one timeline, from a month to two years, plus a week grid across two timezones.
- Filters rename, recolour, hide or block out events by keyword.
- Local lanes: draft events, import `.ics` files, edit and drag to reschedule, export them back.
- An events tray to select, copy, move and download events; a day marker that spans a range of days.
- Your setup travels by share link or a full backup file; kiosk mode locks a shared screen behind a PIN.
- Six colour flavors in light and dark, keyboard shortcuts throughout, and it works offline.
